SOMEWHERE IN OUR FUTURE
My Gulabo,

I don't know exactly what our future will look like.

I don't know which city we'll wake up in, what our home will look like, or what kind of life we'll eventually build together.

There are still so many things we haven't seen, places we haven't been, conversations we haven't had, and memories we haven't made yet.

But there's one thing I quietly hope never changes...

Us. ❤️

I hope years from now, after life has changed us in a hundred different ways, I still look at you and feel that same little warmth in my heart.

I hope we still laugh at the stupidest things, still make plans at midnight, still annoy each other over the smallest things, and still find our way back to each other after difficult days.

Because when I imagine the future, I don't imagine a perfect life.

I imagine a real life...
with you in it.

Forever yours, Akki ❤️
